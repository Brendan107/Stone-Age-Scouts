About Stone Age Scouts

Stone Age Scouts is designed for children aged 7–10. It introduces well-known megalithic sites using short text, large navigation controls, colourful visuals, and simple interactions.

Safe browsing by design
This version does not include clickable links that take children away from the website layout.

What can you do here?

Learn what megaliths are, explore four famous sites, use the interactive map module, take quizzes, and collect simple explorer badges stored safely inside your browser session.
