What are Megaliths?

Megaliths are monuments made with very large stones. People in prehistoric times moved and arranged these stones to make special places. Some were built for burials, ceremonies, or gatherings.

Dolmen

Large upright stones supporting a flat stone on top, often linked with ancient burials.

Menhir

A single large standing stone set upright in the ground.

Stone Circle

A group of standing stones arranged in a ring or circle.

Did you know? Some British megalithic monuments are thousands of years older than the Roman occupation of Britain.
